# Code of conduct

Duskling is a small project, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests or discussions) is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- Not harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately, for any reason.
- Not share anyone's private information without their permission.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who don't follow these rules.

To report a problem, email developer@defec8ed.com. Reports are kept confidential.
